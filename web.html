About

I am a PhD student in Civil and Environmental Engineering at the University of California, Irvine. My research focuses on mathematical modeling, transportation systems, dynamical systems, and data analysis.

I am a graduate researcher in the UCI Robot Ecology Lab and an affiliated doctoral student at the Institute of Transportation Studies. My current work studies traffic assignment, route-choice behavior, and network dynamics.
